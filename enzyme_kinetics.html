Selected starting [S]

Each curve on the product plot is one experiment at a fixed starting [S]. Product forms in a straight line at first, and that slope is v₀, one point on the M-M plot. As substrate runs out the curve bends, so rates are measured early.

Each run adds one point, using the slope over this window. Try a wide range of [S], until the rate levels off, then widen the window and watch the points fall as substrate runs out.

    Determine Vmax and Km

    Estimate from your M-M plot: Vmax is where the rate levels off, and Km is the [S] that gives ½Vmax. Answers count within ±10%.

    For the inhibited curves, enter the apparent values (Vmaxapp and Kmapp).
